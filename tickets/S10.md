As the coffee drinker, I want to search my brews by name, so that I can find a specific brew again quickly.

**Demo:** type "  kenya " in Search: after 250 ms "Kenyan Morning" and "KENYA AA Batch" show, the count reads "2 of 41 brews", a chip "“kenya”" appears and the URL has `q=kenya`. Search "panama natural": "No brews match" with "Clear search and filters".
