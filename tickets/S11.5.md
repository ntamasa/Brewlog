## Tests that prove it
Test-only: no red commit.

**Seam:** store unit (`JournalStore`) · **Setup:** TestBed via the file's `setUp`, `BrewApi` replaced by `{ list }` · **Mocked:** `BrewApi.list` (returns `of(FIRST_PAGE)`)

**Happy path**
1. Given `store.setQuery({ ...DEFAULT_QUERY, sort: 'OLDEST', page: 3 })`, when `store.setSort('HIGHEST')` is called, then `list` was last called with `{ ...DEFAULT_QUERY, sort: 'HIGHEST', page: 1 }`, and `store.query().page` is `1` and `store.query().sort` is `'HIGHEST'` (R8, BREW-48).

**Negative**
2. Given `store.setQuery({ ...DEFAULT_QUERY, q: 'kenya', roast: 'LIGHT', minRating: 4, sort: 'OLDEST', page: 3 })`, when `store.setSort('HIGHEST')` is called, then `list` was last called with `{ ...DEFAULT_QUERY, q: 'kenya', roast: 'LIGHT', minRating: 4, sort: 'HIGHEST', page: 1 }` and `store.query()` equals that same object: the search and filters are not dropped (R8; BREW-48: `...query()`).
