As the coffee drinker, I want to delete methods I no longer use, while "Other" always stays, so that my method list stays tidy and no brew ever loses its method.

**Demo:** trash on "Kalita Wave" (0 brews): "Delete “Kalita Wave”? No brews use it. This can’t be undone."; "Delete method" removes it with the toast "“Kalita Wave” deleted". "Other" shows a lock and "PROTECTED"; `PUT` or delete on Other answers 409. A delete request with stale data still succeeds and moves brews to "Other".
