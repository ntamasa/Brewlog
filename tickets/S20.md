As the coffee drinker, I want the methods dialog and an open brew form to stay correct when methods change elsewhere or while I'm editing, so that I never act on a method that no longer exists.

**Demo:** delete a method in a second tab, then rename it in the first: the toast "That method no longer exists. The list has been refreshed." and the list without it. With the form open on "Moka Pot", "Manage methods…" → delete Moka Pot → "Done": the form keeps its values with "Other" selected.

- **Screens:** `methods` (gone, default), `brew-form` (edit)
