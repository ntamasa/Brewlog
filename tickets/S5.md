As the coffee drinker, I want the brew form to tell me exactly which fields are missing or wrong before anything is saved, so that every brew in my journal is complete and valid.

**Demo:** open "+ Add brew" and click "Add to journal" at once: "6 fields need your attention" with six inline errors. Paste 2,046 characters of notes: the counter "2,046 / 2,000" in error colour. Type the date "2 Nov 2026" on 1 Nov: "The date can’t be in the future". The API rejects the same input with 400 and field codes.
