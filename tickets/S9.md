As the coffee drinker, I want to open a brew and read all of it, including the full tasting notes, so that I can recall exactly how it tasted.

**Demo:** click a card: the detail dialog with Rating "5 of 5", Roast, Method, "Brewed on" and the full notes with their line breaks; "Edit brew" and "Delete" work from there; Esc returns focus to the card.
