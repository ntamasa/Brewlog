## Files that change
- modify `frontend/src/app/journal/journal-page/journal-page.ts`, `.html`, `.scss`: wrap the page in `mat-sidenav-container`; `mat-sidenav mode="over" position="end"` (width `var(--spacing-drawer-width)` = 520 px, `max-width: 100vw` like the prototype's `.drawer`) holds `BrewForm` with `MethodStore.methods()`. - create `e2e/tests/brew-form.spec.ts`.

## Names and signatures
- `JournalPage.openCreate()`: loads methods if `MethodStore.status()` is `idle`, opens the sidenav in create mode. On `saved(brew)`: close, `Toaster.show('“' + brew.name + '” saved to your journal')`, `store.highlight(brew.id)`, `store.reload()`. On `closed`: close the sidenav and return focus to the trigger.
- `BrewCard`'s `highlighted` = `brew.id === store.highlightId()`.

## Tests that prove it
**Seam:** integration (`JournalPage`) · **Setup:** TestBed, real stores and APIs, harnesses · **Mocked:** HTTP (`HttpTestingController`), `MatSnackBar` spied

**Happy path**
1. Given 0 brews (`totalCount` 0), then the count reads "No brews yet", "Your journal is waiting" and "+ Log your first brew" show, and no card, footer or pagination renders (R3).
2. Given the empty state, when "+ Log your first brew" is clicked, then the form opens with the title "Log a brew" and GET `/api/methods` is sent (R3, R12).
3. Given `/journal?page=2` and the form opened via "+ Add brew", when the POST is flushed with brew id 7 ("Kenyan Morning"), then the form closes, the snack bar opens with "“Kenyan Morning” saved to your journal" and `duration: 4000`, and GET `/api/brews?sort=NEWEST&page=2` is sent again; when that reload is flushed with `{"items":[<brew 7 "Kenyan Morning">, <brew 8>], "page":2, "totalPages":2, "matchingCount":14, "totalCount":14}`, then, before the 1200 ms highlight timeout, the card of brew 7 has `brew-card-highlight` and the card of brew 8 doesn't (R12, B24).

**Negative**
4. Given the brews list fails, then the empty state is not shown (the error panel is) (R3).

**Seam:** E2E · **Setup:** compose stack, both projects, `uniqueName('Kenyan Morning')` · **Mocked:** nothing
5. Given `/journal`, when "+ Add brew" (desktop) or the FAB "Add brew" (phone) is clicked, the form "Log a brew" opens in the `mat-sidenav` in both projects (on phone too: no bottom sheet before BREW-82), the fields are filled as in R12 with the unique name, and "Add to journal" is clicked, then the toast "“<name>” saved to your journal" shows and a card with that name is visible; after `docker compose restart backend` (run with `execSync` from the repo root, then poll `GET /api/brews` until 200) and a page reload, the card is still there (R12).
