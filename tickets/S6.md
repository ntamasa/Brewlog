As the coffee drinker, I want the form to handle duplicate names, deleted methods, server failures and accidental closing without losing what I typed, so that saving a brew is always safe.

**Demo:** save a second "kenyan morning": the duplicate-name error, values kept. Stop the backend and save: "Couldn’t save your brew", values kept. Change the name: its error clears, while the banner stays until the next save. Type something and press Esc: "Discard changes?".
