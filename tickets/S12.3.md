The sheet moves no focus itself when it opens and uses no `cdkTrapFocus`: `MatBottomSheet` focuses × (R47).
- The picker is bound with `[ngModel]="query().minRating ?? 1"` and `(ngModelChange)="onMinRating($event)"` (`FormsModule`). `onMinRating(v: number)`: `store.setFilter({ minRating: v === 1 ? null : v })`. `minRating` 1 is never sent. Tapping the already selected bean changes nothing.
- Keys (R46): the sheet adds no key handling of its own. `BeanRating` turns ArrowRight/ArrowUp and ArrowLeft/ArrowDown into value changes (bean 1 → 2, never below 1 or above 5) and emits nothing when the value stays the same. So from "Any rating", ArrowRight gives `minRating` 2, ArrowLeft does nothing, and at "5 beans only" ArrowRight does nothing.
- `minRatingLegend(minRating: number | null): string`, exported from `filters-sheet.ts`: null → "Any rating", 2–4 → "<n>+ beans", 5 → "5 beans only".
