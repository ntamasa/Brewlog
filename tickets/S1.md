As the coffee drinker, I want a landing page that explains Brewlog and leads into my journal, so that I know what the app is for and can start using it.

**Demo:** `docker compose up -d --build --wait`, open http://localhost:8081: the landing page with its three photos and credits; "Open my journal" goes to `/journal` (still an empty page). The backend starts with the seeded methods in PostgreSQL, and the backend, frontend and e2e CI workflows run.

This first story also sets up the whole repository (frontend, backend, Docker, CI, end-to-end tests) that every later story builds on.
