As the coffee drinker, I want my search, filters, sort and page kept in the URL, so that reloading, going back or sharing a link brings me back to the same view, and a broken link still works.

**Demo:** open `/journal?q=kenya&roast=LIGHT&minRating=4&sort=HIGHEST&page=2`: the toolbar shows those values and page 2 loads; Back and reload keep it. Change any filter on page 3: page 1. Open `/journal?roast=FOO&methodId=9`: the params disappear and all brews show. `GET /api/brews?roast=FOO` answers 400.
