As the coffee drinker, I want to edit a brew I logged, so that I can correct it or add what I noticed later.

**Demo:** on a card, ⋮ → "Edit brew": the prefilled form. Change the rating to 4 and "Save changes": only `{"rating": 4}` is sent, the toast and the highlighted card. Delete the brew in another tab and save again: "This brew no longer exists" with "Save as new brew".

## Design
- **Screens:** `brew-form` (edit, brew-gone), `brews` (saved, card-menu)
- **Screenshots:** attached, all new: `brew-form.edit.*.png`, `brew-form.brew-gone.*.png`, `brews.card-menu.*.png` (desktop and mobile). `brews.saved` and `brew-form.saving` (the busy "Saving…" that "Save changes" and "Save as new brew" show too) are attached to the story "Log a brew".
