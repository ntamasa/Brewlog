As the coffee drinker, I want to sort my brews by date or rating, so that I can see my latest or my best brews first.

**Demo:** with A (1 Nov, 4, added first), B (1 Nov, 4, added second) and C (30 Oct, 5): Newest first B, A, C; Oldest first C, A, B; Highest rated C, B, A; Lowest rated B, A, C.
