As the coffee drinker, I want to log a brew with its bean, origin, method, roast, rating, date and notes, so that it's kept in my journal.

**Demo:** with an empty journal, open `/journal`: "Your journal is waiting". Click "+ Log your first brew", fill in Kenyan Morning and click "Add to journal": the toast "“Kenyan Morning” saved to your journal", the highlighted card, and the brew is still there after `docker compose restart backend`.

- **Screens:** `brew-form` (create, saving, method-open), `brews` (saved, empty)
