As the coffee drinker with a big journal, I want the pagination to stay short however many pages there are, so that I can still jump to the first, last and nearby pages.

**Demo:** with 240 brews, open `/journal?page=10` on desktop: "240 brews", "Showing 109–120 of 240" and ‹ 1 … 9 10 11 … 20 ›. With exactly 7 pages every number shows. On a phone the same page reads ‹ PAGE 10 OF 20 ›.
