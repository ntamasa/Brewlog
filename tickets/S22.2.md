## Names and signatures
- `SheetOrDialog.open<T, R>(component: Type<T>, config: { data?: unknown; width: string; panelClass?: string }): { afterClosed(): Observable<R | undefined> }`: `Viewport.isPhone()` → `MatBottomSheet.open(component, { data, autoFocus: '[cdkFocusInitial]', panelClass: ['brewlog-sheet', …] })`; else `MatDialog.open(component, { data, width, autoFocus: '[cdkFocusInitial]', panelClass })`. Both keep `restoreFocus` at its default, true (R47).
- The form's phone sheet is opened with `autoFocus: '[cdkFocusInitial]'` as well, next to its `disableClose: true`, so it opens with focus on Brew name and returns focus to the FAB or "+ Log your first brew" when it closes (R47). Nothing in this subtask calls `focus()` or uses `cdkTrapFocus`.
- `injectOverlayData<D>(): D` and `injectOverlayRef<R>(): { close(result?: R): void }`: read `MAT_BOTTOM_SHEET_DATA`/`MatBottomSheetRef` or `MAT_DIALOG_DATA`/`MatDialogRef`, whichever is present (`inject(..., { optional: true })`).

## Tests that prove it
**Seam:** integration (`JournalPage`) · **Setup:** TestBed, real stores, fake `BreakpointObserver`; focus tests like those in `journal-page.spec.ts` (BREW-23: `MATERIAL_ANIMATIONS` disabled, the trigger focused with its harness's `focus()` before `click()`, `vi.waitFor` on `document.activeElement`, and the FAB first shown with `showFab(page)`, because jsdom's fixed 1024 px viewport hides it through `.mobile-only`) · **Mocked:** HTTP, breakpoints

**Happy path**
1. Given phone, when a card is clicked, then the detail opens in a `mat-bottom-sheet-container`; when "Manage methods" is tapped, the methods list opens in a bottom sheet; when the FAB is tapped, the form ("Log a brew") opens in a bottom sheet (R42).
2. Given phone and the form sheet, when a valid brew is saved, then the sheet closes and the toast shows: `Toaster.show` is called with "“Kenyan Morning” saved to your journal" (the `Toaster` fake of `journal-page.spec.ts` since BREW-104) (R42).
3. Given desktop, then the detail and methods open in `mat-dialog-container` and the form in the sidenav (R42); the detail opens with focus on × "Close" and the methods dialog with focus on its × "Close" (R47).
4. Given phone and the FAB focused, when it is tapped, then the form sheet opens with focus on the "Brew name" input; when "Cancel" is tapped, then the sheet closes and focus is on the FAB (R47).
5. Given phone and 0 brews, when the focused "+ Log your first brew" is tapped, then the form sheet opens with focus on "Brew name"; after "Cancel", focus is on "+ Log your first brew" (R47).
6. Given phone and a card's name button focused, when it is tapped, then the detail sheet opens with focus on × "Close"; when × is tapped, then the sheet closes and focus is on that card's name button (R47, R26).
7. Given phone, when "Manage methods" is tapped, then the methods sheet opens with focus on its × "Close" (R47).

**Negative**
8. Given phone and a changed form, when the sheet backdrop is tapped, then the sheet stays open with "Discard changes?" (`disableClose: true` on the sheet) (R42).
9. Given phone and the form sheet opened via the focused FAB with the origin changed, when "Cancel" and then "Discard" are tapped, then the sheet closes and focus is on the FAB, not on `document.body` (R47, R21).
10. Given phone and each of the three sheets just opened (form, detail, methods), then focus isn't on the `mat-bottom-sheet-container` itself, and the sheet contains exactly one `[cdkFocusInitial]` element (R47).
11. Busy: given phone and the form sheet with a valid brew, when "Add to journal" is tapped and the POST isn't flushed yet, then the sheet's primary reads "Saving…" with `aria-busy="true"` and `aria-disabled="true"`, "Cancel" has neither `disabled` nor `aria-disabled`, and a second tap leaves exactly one POST `/api/brews` (R48, B26).
