As the coffee drinker, I want to see that my journal is loading, and a clear message with a retry when it can't load, so that I'm never left looking at a blank page.

**Demo:** with the backend stopped, open `/journal`: skeleton cards, then "We couldn’t load your brews"; start the backend and click "Try again": the brews appear.

## Design
- **Screens:** `brews` (loading, error)
- **Screenshots:** attached. `brews.loading.*.png` and `brews.error.*.png` (desktop and mobile) are the new design.
