As the coffee drinker, I want to filter my brews by method, roast and minimum rating, combined with search and sort, so that I can narrow the journal down to the brews I'm looking for.

**Demo:** pick Method "Pour Over", Roast "Light", Min. rating "4+" and Sort "Highest rated": exactly the 3 matching brews, "3 of 41 brews", chips for method, roast and rating. On a phone: "Filters" with the badge "3" (with a search too: "4"), the "Filter & sort" sheet with its bean picker ("Any rating" at bean 1, "4+ beans" at bean 4) and "Show 3 brews".
