As the coffee drinker, I want to delete a brew after confirming it, so that my journal only keeps the brews I want.

**Demo:** ⋮ → "Delete brew" → "Delete this brew?" with focus on "Keep brew"; "Delete brew" removes it, shows "Brew deleted", and the list stays on the same page (or the previous one if the page became empty).
