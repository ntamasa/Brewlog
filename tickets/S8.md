As the coffee drinker, I want to delete a brew after confirming it, so that my journal only keeps the brews I want.

**Demo:** ⋮ → "Delete brew" → "Delete this brew?" with focus on "Keep brew"; "Delete brew" shows "Deleting…" in its red fill at 75% while the request runs (a second click sends nothing), then removes it, shows "Brew deleted", and the list stays on the same page (or the previous one if the page became empty).

## Design
- **Screens:** `brew-delete` (default, deleting, error, gone)
- **Screenshots:** attached, all new: `brew-delete.default.*.png`, `brew-delete.deleting.*.png`, `brew-delete.error.*.png`, `brew-delete.gone.*.png` (desktop and mobile). `brew-delete.deleting.*.png` is the busy "Deleting…" (review of PR #6); `brew-delete.deleting.*_deprecated.png` is the look before.
