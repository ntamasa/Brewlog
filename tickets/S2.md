As the coffee drinker, I want to see my brews as cards in my journal, newest first and 12 per page, so that I can look back at what I tasted.

**Demo:** with 41 brews in the database, open `/journal`: "41 brews", 12 cards newest first, "Showing 1–12 of 41", pages 1–4; clicking page 4 shows the last 5 brews.
