As the coffee drinker, I want to see which brews a rename affects and confirm it first, so that I never rename a method on many brews by accident.

**Demo:** rename "Pour Over" (14 brews) to "Pour Over (V60)": the step view "Rename “Pour Over” to “Pour Over (V60)”?" lists all 14 brews newest first; "Rename for all 14 brews" saves, all 14 cards show "Pour Over (V60)" with the pour-over icon. "Cancel" goes back to the input with the typed name.
