## Files that change
- modify `frontend/src/app/landing/landing-page/landing-page.ts`: `imports: [RouterLink, MatButtonModule, MatIconModule]`.
- modify `frontend/src/app/landing/landing-page/landing-page.html`: markup and copy from `landing.html`. Icons as `<mat-icon svgIcon="…">`, images from `images/*.jpg`. The three CTAs are Material buttons on `<a>`, never the prototype's `btn` classes:
  - hero "Open my journal": `<a matButton="filled" class="btn-accent" routerLink="/journal">`
  - hero "How it works": `<a matButton="outlined" class="btn-on-scrim" routerLink="/" fragment="how">`
  - closing "Open my journal →": `<a matButton="filled" routerLink="/journal">Open my journal <mat-icon svgIcon="arrow-right" iconPositionEnd aria-hidden="true"></mat-icon></a>`
  - The nav "Open journal →" stays the prototype's plain `a.nav-link` and the brand marks stay plain `a.brand` links: not buttons.
- modify `frontend/src/app/landing/landing-page/landing-page.scss`: the `<style>` block of `landing.html` with its media queries, its `.btn` selectors rewritten to `.mdc-button` (`.hero .ctas .mdc-button { flex: 1; }`, `.closing .mdc-button { width: 100%; }`), plus the matching non-button parts of `components.css`. Never the prototype's `.btn`, `.btn-*`, `.icon-btn` or `.fab` rules (`components.css` lines 44–75), no button `:hover` rule, no `::ng-deep`, and no change to `theme.scss`.

1. Given `/` is rendered, then it contains the texts "Remember every cup worth remembering.", "A ledger for the cups you love.", "Brew. Log. Rediscover." and "Your next great cup starts with the last one." (R1).
2. Given `/`, then three `img` elements have `src` ending in `hero-pour-over.jpg`, `beans.jpg` and `cup-notebook.jpg`, each with a non-empty `alt`, and the footer contains the three photo credits from `landing.html` (R1).
3. Given `/`, when the hero "Open my journal" is clicked, then the URL is `/journal`; the same for the nav "Open journal →" and the closing "Open my journal →" (R1).
4. Given `/`, when "How it works" is clicked, then the URL is `/#how` and an element with `id="how"` exists (R1).
6. Given `/`, then `MatButtonHarness.with({ ancestor: '.hero', text: 'Open my journal' })` has `getAppearance()` `'filled'` and its host has the class `btn-accent`; `MatButtonHarness.with({ text: 'How it works' })` has `'outlined'` and the class `btn-on-scrim`; `MatButtonHarness.with({ ancestor: '.closing' })` has `'filled'`, no class `btn-accent`, and contains a `mat-icon` with `data-mat-icon-name="arrow-right"` and the attribute `iconPositionEnd` (R1).

**Negative**
5. Given the page scrolled down, when the brand mark is clicked, then the URL stays `/` with no fragment (R1).
7. Given `/`, then exactly 3 elements have the class `mdc-button` (the nav "Open journal →" and both brand marks are not Material buttons), and no element has any of the classes `btn`, `btn-primary`, `btn-ghost`, `icon-btn`, `fab` (R1).
