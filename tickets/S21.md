As the coffee drinker, I want every request for a brew or method that doesn't exist, and every malformed request, to get a clear answer, so that the app never breaks in an unexpected way.

**Demo:** `PATCH /api/brews/999`, `DELETE /api/brews/999` → 404 `BREW_NOT_FOUND`; `PUT /api/methods/999`, `POST /api/methods/999/delete`, `GET /api/methods/999/brews` → 404 `METHOD_NOT_FOUND`; a malformed JSON body → 400 without field errors, and the screen shows its generic server-error banner.
