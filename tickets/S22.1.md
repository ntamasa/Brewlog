**Goal:** Add one breakpoint service and the phone pagination, and check the grid columns at each width.

**Read first:**
- Story → Frontend (Breakpoints), Acceptance criteria (R42)
- `design/DESIGN.md` (layout, breakpoints), `design/screens/brews.md` (Layout 5–6), `design/prototype/screens/brews.html` (phone footer), `design/screenshots/brews.default.mobile.png`, `design/screenshots/brews.default.desktop.png`

## Files that change
- create `frontend/src/app/core/viewport.ts` (+ spec).
- modify `frontend/src/app/journal/pagination/pagination.ts`, `.html`, `.scss`, `.spec.ts`.
- modify `frontend/src/app/journal/journal-toolbar/journal-toolbar.ts`: use `Viewport.isPhone` instead of its own `BreakpointObserver` query.

## Names and signatures
- `Viewport` (`@Injectable({ providedIn: 'root' })`): `isPhone = toSignal(breakpointObserver.observe('(max-width: 767.98px)').pipe(map(r => r.matches)))`, `isDesktop` for `'(min-width: 1120px)'`.
- `Pagination` on phone: full width, ‹ button (aria-label "Previous page"), text "PAGE <n> OF <total>" (`label-caps`), › button (aria-label "Next page"); same disabled rules; footer stacks above it.

## Tests that prove it
**Seam:** component · **Setup:** TestBed, `BreakpointObserver` replaced by a fake emitting the chosen match · **Mocked:** breakpoints

**Happy path**
1. Given phone, page 1 of 4, then the pagination reads "PAGE 1 OF 4" with ‹ disabled and › enabled, and no page-number buttons (R42).
2. Given desktop, page 1 of 4, then the numbered buttons 1–4 render (R42).

**Negative**
3. Given phone, page 4 of 4, then › is disabled; clicking ‹ emits 3 (R42).
