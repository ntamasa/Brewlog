As the coffee drinker, I want to see my brew methods and add my own, so that every brew can name the method I really used.

**Demo:** "Manage methods" opens "Brew methods": Espresso, Pour Over, French Press, AeroPress, Moka Pot, Cold Brew with their brew counts, then "Other" locked. Add " Chemex ": the row "Chemex · 0 brews · added by you" is highlighted, the toast "“Chemex” added", and Chemex appears in the form's method list and the Method filter. "pour over" gives the duplicate error. With the backend stopped: "We couldn’t load your methods" with "Try again".
