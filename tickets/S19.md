As the coffee drinker, I want to choose where each brew of a deleted method goes, "Other" by default, so that deleting a method never loses track of how I made a brew.

**Demo:** trash on "Moka Pot" (3 brews): "Delete “Moka Pot”?" lists the 3 brews on "Other", summary "3 to Other". Set one to AeroPress ("2 to Other · 1 to AeroPress"), "Delete method": the toast "“Moka Pot” deleted · 3 brews reassigned", counts Other +2, AeroPress +1, and the journal reloads.
